# World of Tanks EU Store catalog

Observed in the official EU in-game Store on 18 November 2026, on client
`v.2.4.1.1 #993`. This is a reference for the live game. The
[server's Store implementation](store.md) has its own catalog.

The catalog covers the Store opened from the garage. It excludes Event Rewards,
Battle Pass and Onslaught season offers, weekly and seasonal merchandise,
dated promotions, and special discount prices. Fixed package prices with a
standing quantity or duration saving are retained. For undated cosmetic packs
that show a crossed-out amount, the tables record that reference amount and
label it as such. It can be the sum of the contents' prices. A Store card
marked **In Garage** or **In Depot** can hide the price and, in the main
Vehicles tab, ownership alone does not establish current sale eligibility.

## Sources and verification

- Live official EU Store, English UI. Price labels are the amounts shown before
  checkout. EUR amounts reflect the inspected session. Garage ownership masks
  some prices.
- The inspected account also displayed **Unlimited** credit prices for
  Firefly VC (616,000), Chieftain/T95 (1,386,000), M4A1 FL 10 (726,000)
  and Turán III PT (374,000). The Chieftain/T95 preview showed **Recover**.
  These account-specific recovery cards are omitted from the sale tables.
- Installed `res/packages/scripts.pkg`, entries
  `scripts/item_defs/vehicles/<nation>/list.xml` and
  `scripts/item_defs/vehicles/common/optional_devices.xml`. The
  `collectorVehicle` tag produced the same counts per nation as the live
  Collectors’ cards. The first nine German prices matched the live cards.
  Equipment classes use the packaged base prices, verified against the Store's
  displayed starting prices.
- Names from installed `res/text/lc_messages/<nation>_vehicles.mo`, including
  `gb_vehicles.mo` for the U.K., and `artefacts.mo`.
- `WorldOfTanks.exe` SHA-256
  `fc80a8fcd804dba1ce54482d87950f4cbe31b3742eefd0c3de03dc28e941af7c`.
  Running `win64/WorldOfTanks.exe` SHA-256
  `58325ded3e2db7134638971e33de1eab09737f8b66401554d9723a2bf246bb11`.
